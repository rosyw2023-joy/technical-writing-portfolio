# Guest Check-Out Guide

## Overview

The Guest Check-Out feature enables hotel staff to complete a guest's stay, process any outstanding payments, and update the room status for future bookings.

## Prerequisites

Before checking out a guest, ensure that:

* The guest is currently checked in.
* All room charges have been recorded.
* Any additional services, such as room service or laundry, have been added to the guest's account.
* Outstanding balances have been reviewed.

## Check-Out Procedure

1. Log in to StaySync.
2. Open the **Reservations** module.
3. Search for the guest using their name or reservation number.
4. Open the guest's reservation details.
5. Review all charges and confirm the final balance.
6. Process the final payment, if required.
7. Click **Check Out** to complete the process.

## Expected Result

After a successful check-out:

* The guest's reservation is marked as **Completed**.
* The room status changes to **Available** or **Pending Housekeeping**, depending on hotel policy.
* The invoice is finalized and stored in the system.

> **Note:** Ensure all payments are completed before checking out the guest.

> **Tip:** Confirm the guest's departure date and time before completing the check-out process to avoid billing errors.
# Payment Processing Guide

## Overview

The Payment Processing feature enables hotel staff to settle a guest's outstanding balance before check-out. It ensures that all charges are recorded accurately and that the guest receives proof of payment upon completion of the transaction.

## Prerequisites

Before processing a payment, ensure that:

* The guest is ready to check out.
* All room charges and additional services have been added to the guest's account.
* Any room damages or extra fees have been assessed and included in the final bill.

## Payment Procedure

1. Review the guest's account and confirm all charges are accurate.
2. If any room damages or additional charges apply, add them to the guest's final bill.
3. Generate the final invoice and present it to the guest.
4. Ask the guest to choose their preferred payment method (cash, card, mobile money, or any other method supported by the hotel).
5. Process the payment and confirm that the transaction is successful.
6. Issue an official receipt to the guest.
7. Thank the guest for staying at the hotel and invite them to visit again.

## Expected Result

After a successful payment:

* The guest's account balance is cleared.
* A payment receipt is generated.
* The reservation is ready for the check-out process.

> **Note:** Verify the final bill with the guest before processing the payment.

> **Tip:** Always issue a receipt immediately after payment to maintain accurate financial records and improve the guest experience.
